Chirp

Any speaker can talk to your glasses.

Display glasses can show and hear, but they can't see: no camera, no NFC, no Bluetooth scan. Chirp turns a short, soft sound into the way they receive things. A phone, a laptop or a stream plays a chirp; the glasses hear it and the right card appears on the lens. One tap, and it lands in Telegram.

On the glasses

Open Chirp, send the 6-digit code on your lens to the bot, and keep the app open. Tap acts on a card, double-tap picks the other option, swipe goes back, long press pauses listening. Everything you heard is in /heard.
