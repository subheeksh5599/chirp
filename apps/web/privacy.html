ChirpAll tools

Privacy

Microphone (glasses and phone)

The glasses app listens for Chirp tones. Audio stays in memory on your phone, is decoded on the spot, and is dropped. Nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded. Only a decoded 9-character code is sent to our server. A long press on the glasses pauses listening. The phone microphone is used only if you switch to it.

Network

The app talks to one server, the Chirp API at the same domain as this page, hosted on Vercel with a Neon Postgres database.

What we store

  • Your Telegram user id, name and username, once you pair, so we can send what you heard to your Telegram chat.
  • What your glasses heard (talk titles, contact cards, bills, markets) for your /heard log.
  • Room codes heard during a session, to work out presence. Presence records name you only by a one-way pseudonym.
  • Votes, contacts you chose to connect with, paper positions, and testnet payment references.

Speaker subtitles

If a speaker turns on subtitles, 5-second clips from the speaker’s own microphone are sent to a speech-to-text service (Groq) and the latest line is shown to listeners. Listener audio is never sent.

Money

Chirp holds no funds and never signs anything. Payments and anchors are TON testnet transactions you confirm in your own wallet. Paper positions are not trades.
